Finance & Payment Options

No card details, no online payment — we've built our whole checkout around Cash on Delivery.

💷 Cash on Delivery

Order online or via WhatsApp, and pay the full amount in cash when your furniture is delivered to your door. Nothing to pay upfront.

🗓️ Spreading the Cost

For larger orders, message our team on WhatsApp before you order — we're happy to talk through options for paying in instalments on delivery.

🔒 No Card Details, Ever

We never ask for card details online or over the phone. If anyone contacts you asking for card details on Furnecia's behalf, please let us know immediately.

🧾 Simple Returns

Because there's no online payment to refund, returns are simple too — return within 30 days and we'll arrange collection.
