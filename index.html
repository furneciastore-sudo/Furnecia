Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer Cash on Delivery across the UK?

Yes. We accept Cash on Delivery on orders across England, Scotland and Wales. You pay in cash when your order arrives.

How long does delivery take?

Most in-stock items ship within 3–5 working days. Exact timing can vary slightly by product and location — you will see an estimate on each product page.

Do you assemble furniture?

Most items arrive flat-packed with clear step-by-step instructions for self-assembly. If you would like assembly help, get in touch and we will let you know what is available in your area.

What's your returns policy?

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for an exchange. Please contact us before returning anything so we can arrange collection.

Do you deliver to Scotland / Northern Ireland?

We deliver to Scotland, with the delivery cost calculated at checkout. We do not currently deliver to Northern Ireland or the Republic of Ireland.

Can I pay by card on delivery?

No — we currently only accept Cash on Delivery. We do not take card payments or bank transfers at the point of delivery or at checkout.
