About Furnecia

Affordable furniture, delivered across the UK

Furnecia is a UK furniture store specialising in sofa beds, corner sofas, wardrobes and bedroom furniture — designed to make good quality furniture affordable and easy to buy. We know that furnishing a home is a big decision, which is why we offer Cash on Delivery on every order: you only pay once your furniture has arrived safely at your door.

What we offer

From space-saving sofa beds to full bedroom wardrobe sets, every item in our range is chosen for comfort, durability and value. Most items ship within 3–5 working days, and we back every order with a 30-day return policy for peace of mind.

Cash on Delivery, made simple

No card details, no online payment, no waiting on bank transfers — just place your order, and pay in cash when it's delivered. It's that straightforward.
